# concordia-terra-crowd

Concordia's crowds: navigation agents (`NavAgent` plus a `CrowdAgent`) steered together by Terra processors in the deterministic parallel scheduler, following DetourCrowd's steps over Detour queries. Each tick the crowd snapshots every agent and character into a spatial grid, keeps each agent's polygon corridor (following its body, shortcutting toward visible corners), finds its nearest neighbours, steers toward its corners (slowing at the end, with optional separation), picks a velocity that avoids its neighbours and nearby walls (DetourCrowd's sampled velocity obstacles), accelerates, pushes overlapping agents apart, and moves surface agents along the navmesh (or hands physics agents' velocities to their `CharacterController`). Crowd agents plan through `nav.plan`'s budget and raise the same `NavArrived` and `NavPathFailed` signals as plain agents, `ClickToMove` drives them, templates add them with the `Crowd` trait, and the `crowd` debug layer draws them (walls included). Far agents opt into simulation LOD: they choose their corners and velocities less often but still collide and arrive. **Traffic** drives vehicles along lanes and junctions placed as scene entities: each vehicle keeps a safe gap with the Intelligent Driver Model, stops at junctions until it is let in (higher-priority roads first), brakes for people standing in its lane, and either wanders or drives to a destination lane. The `lanes` debug layer draws it all. Enabled by `[nav]` in `concordia.toml`. `benches/BASELINE.md` records a tick of 10,000 agents.

See `AGENTS.md` for invariants, tests and references.
