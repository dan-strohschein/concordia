# Assets of Lamplight (M2's sample game)

Every binary file under `assets/` has one row: where it came from, its licence (an SPDX id that
`deny.toml` allows) and its SHA-256. `tests/integration/lamplight_content.rs` checks the table
against the files and re-runs our generators to check their pins (a regeneration that differs is
a review item, not a silent change: the Ogg and glTF bytes also depend on the oggenc and Blender
versions, vorbis-tools 1.4.3 and Blender 5.2.1 here).

- Third-party files are CC0 1.0 packs by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), copied unchanged from the
  archives named below after the author reviewed the picks (2026-11-23). Crediting is not
  required by CC0; we credit Kenney gladly. The town models share one palette texture,
  `town/Textures/colormap.png`, which their glTF files reference by that relative path.
- Our own files are written by the scripts in `../tools/` (CC0 1.0). The characters reuse the
  repository's original mannequin from `tools/fixtures/make_fbx.py`.
- The text content (`../materials`, `../anim`, `../audio`, `../ui`, `../input`) was written for
  Concordia and is CC0 1.0. Nothing here carries a third-party mark in its name (decision D8).
