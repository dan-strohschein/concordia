# m4-lighting

Reference scenes for M4's lighting (phase 4.7 onward), compared against the path tracer
(`concordia-pathtrace`, ADR-0090, ADR-0101). Everything is generated by our code; nothing is
downloaded.

- **`sun_courtyard`** (4.7a): a walled courtyard with an archway and three windows, a pergola of
  sixteen thin slats over four columns, a bench on thin legs, a lattice screen, a pole, an urn and
  a rock (the urn and rock are dense enough for the meshlet path). One low sun with shadows, **no
  sky and no emission**, so its direct light is the sun's alone: the path tracer's direct-only
  references (`max_bounces: 1`) and the renderer's image show the same light, and their
  difference is shadow error.
- References: `tests/golden/m4_reference/sun_courtyard_iso` (an isometric-like three-quarter view
  from high up, narrow field of view) and `sun_courtyard_ground` (the scene camera, low).

The `.glb` files are not in git. Each sidecar records its recipe (provider, version, request,
seed) and the BLAKE3 of the bytes it makes; `gen.restore` remakes missing files and checks them.
Rebuild the scene with `just lighting-build`.
